Questions from the fireside

Is the free share really free?

Yes — claimFree() costs only network gas. One per wallet, 300 in total, and the contract enforces both limits itself.

Where does the money go?

100% of every buy() payment is forwarded to the treasury inside the same transaction. The contract keeps nothing and has no withdrawal function.

What happens to unsold shares?

They are burned. After 24 Nov 00:30 WIB anyone can call finalize(), which destroys every FABL still held by the contract.

Can the story be rewritten?

No. The price, caps, window and treasury are constants in a verified, immutable contract. There is no owner who could change a single word.
